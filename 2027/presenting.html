Presenting at RLC 2027

New this year, authors with papers accepted to the Reinforcement Learning Journal (RLJ) will be able to present their papers at one of the two locations for RLC 2027. There is no longer a separate paper submission and review process for RLC.

Here's how the new process will work. Authors submit papers to RLJ which accepts papers on a rolling basis. Instructions for submission to RLJ can be found on the RLJ submission page. Papers that are accepted to RLJ by May 1 will be eligible for presentation at RLC 2027.

We want to emphasize that papers must be accepted by RLJ---not just submitted---by a certain date. The RLJ editors are aspiring to return decisions on papers within 8 weeks of submission but cannot guarantee this timeline, particularly if many authors submit around the same time. Furthermore, the initial RLJ decision may be to revise the submitted article, which could further delay an acceptance decision. We strongly encourage authors to submit well in advance to maximize the chance that they receive a decision in time to present.

Authors of accepted papers will be able to choose which location(s) they present at. The exact type of presentations will be set by the program chairs and announced after the acceptance cut-off date. We anticipate all accepted papers having at least a poster presentation.
