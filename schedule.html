WiML Networking Lunch

Women in Machine Learning (WiML) will host an informal networking lunch during RLC, giving attendees a chance to meet others in the community, make new connections, and take a social break together during the conference.

Pick up your lunch from RLC and bring it with you to the gathering.

Plan for the hour
12:00–12:25 PM — Welcome + Networking
Grab your RLC lunch and meet at the WiML gathering point for a brief welcome, an introduction to WiML, and informal networking with conversation prompts.
12:25–12:45 PM — Interactive Wooclap Trivia
Join a fun, interactive trivia game designed as an icebreaker and an easy way to get the whole group engaging together.
12:45–1:00 PM — Open Networking
Continue conversations, exchange contact information, and connect around research, mentorship, collaboration, and the broader ML/RL community.

The event is casual—simply bring your conference lunch and join us!
