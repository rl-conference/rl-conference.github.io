FAQ

How do I connect to Wi‑Fi on campus?

RLC ’26 is hosted on the Université de Montréal (UdeM) campus. You can use either of these networks:

eduroam — Available to most students and academic affiliates through their home institution.

UdeM-Visiteurs — For guests without eduroam access: select UdeM-Visiteurs in your Wi‑Fi settings, choose Or register for guest access at the bottom, and complete the short registration form.

Can I attend virtually?

No.

Can I present remotely if I cannot travel?

There are no plans for remote attendance infrastructure or livestreaming of sessions. If you cannot travel, you may be able to present by recording a talk video and sending it to your session chair to play during the session. For posters, the best option is to arrange for a co-author or colleague to present on your behalf.

Will talks be recorded?

Keynotes will be recorded and shared after the conference. Paper presentations will not be recorded.

What is the format for paper presentations?

Every accepted paper is given a five-minute talk followed by one minute for questions. In addition, all papers have a slot in a poster session.

Presentation slots are available on the paper schedule. Please check it for your assigned talk time, room, and poster session.

What are the poster dimensions?

Posters can be up to 48" wide by 36" tall (about 122 cm by 91 cm).

Does workshop-only registration include the banquet or Cirque du Soleil?

No, it does not.
